# Menu Board - Quick Card for the Store

Keep this next to the register. No computer skills needed.

## Every day

**Turn the PC on. That's it.**
The menus appear on both TVs by themselves after about a minute.

## Change a price

1. Go to the PC (the small screen, not the TVs).
2. If you don't see the "Menu Board Admin" window, press the **Esc** key.
3. On the left, click **TV 1** (hot food) or **TV 2** (drinks & snacks).
4. Click the category, then click the price you want to change.
5. Type the new price and click anywhere else.

The TV updates **instantly**. There is no Save button - it saves by itself, and it remembers everything even after the power goes out.

## Something is sold out

Find the item the same way, and **uncheck the box that says "Available."**
It disappears from the TV. Check the box again when it's back.

## The TVs are blank

1. Is the PC turned on? Turn it on and wait a minute.
2. Still blank? **Restart the PC** (Start button → Power → Restart) and wait a minute.
3. Still blank? Check the TV is on and set to the right input (HDMI).

That fixes it almost every time. Nothing is lost - the menu is saved on the PC.

## Do not

- Do not close the "Menu Board Admin" window with the X. If you do by accident, it will ask "Exit Menu Board?" - click **No**.
- Do not unplug the PC while the store is open.
